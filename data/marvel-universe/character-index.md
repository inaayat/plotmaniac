Every character heading on both pages is included, in the order it appears on Wikipedia. Where a heading follows the "Real Name / Superhero Name" pattern (e.g. *Bruce Banner / Hulk*), the two are split into separate columns. Where a character has no separate alias, the name sits in **Real Name** and **Superhero/Alias Name** is left blank. Cells are left blank wherever the source page didn't give an actor or a clear list of appearances — rows are still included rather than dropped.
